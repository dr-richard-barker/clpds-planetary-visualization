\section{Conclusion}

The \textbf{CLPDS Planetary Exploration Data Visualization Suite} provides a unified, FAIR-compliant software environment for exploring in-situ and orbital observations from China's lunar and Martian exploration missions. By bridging subsurface radar stratigraphy, mineralogical spectroscopy, in-situ water detection, and environmental timeseries into an intuitive, high-performance WebGL interface, this platform empowers researchers across planetary geology, space biology, and astronautics to extract cross-mission insights. As upcoming missions such as Chang'e-7 (lunar south pole volatile survey), Chang'e-8 (lunar base testbed), and Tianwen-3 (Mars sample return) expand humanity's deep space footprint, open data systems and interactive visualization pipelines will serve as vital infrastructure for discovery and international collaboration.
